# Books And Book Chapters

`books_note.md` records three important book/book chapter contributions.

## Relief Supply Chain Management For Disasters (2012)

- Chapter: “Relief Supply Chain Planning: Insights from Thailand”.
- Authors include Ruth Banomyong and Apichat Sopadang/Sodapang.
- Theme: humanitarian logistics, disaster response, simulation-based relief supply chain planning.

## Industry 4.0 For SMEs (2020)

- Chapter: “Smart SME 4.0 Implementation Toolkit”.
- Authors: Apichat Sopadang, Nilubon Chonsawat, Sakgasem Ramingwong.
- Theme: SME readiness toward SME 4.0 using Thai SMEs as case study.

## Implementing Industry 4.0 In SMEs (2021)

- Chapter: “Implementation Strategies for SME 4.0: Insights on Thailand”.
- Authors: Apichat Sopadang, Sakgasem Ramingwong, Tanyanuparb Anantana, Krisana Tamvimol.
- Theme: strategies and pathways for Thai SMEs to become SME 4.0.

## Narrative Link

These sources support a PI narrative built on resilience, digital readiness, SME access/adoption, and implementation governance.
