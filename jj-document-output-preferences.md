# Document Output Preferences

## Thai Word Reports

- Use Bai Jamjuree font for Thai reports when asked.
- Word files should be polished and preserve tables/layout when source content matters.
- For executive briefs, user often asks for “รายงานสั้น 1 หน้า” or Word file.
- For EdPEx/SAR work, do not reduce full drafts into only structural summaries unless explicitly requested.

## Common Output Types

- 1-page executive briefing.
- Literature map.
- Thai Word report.
- Spreadsheet model.
- Policy brief.
- EdPEx assessment report.

## Quality Rule

Verify source documents in cache/upload folders before reporting missing files.
